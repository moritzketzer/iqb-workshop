# Figure 1 — Piebald Guinea-Pig Path Diagram

**File**: `wright-1920-pnas-fig1-piebald-guinea-pig-path-diagram.png`
**Page**: ~323

## Description

Wright's original path diagram applying the method of path coefficients to heredity in guinea pigs. Shows the causal structure linking sire (H''), dam (H'''), genetic factors (G, G', G'', G'''), and offspring coat pattern (O, O'), with environmental (E) and chance inputs. One of the earliest published causal DAGs.

## Source

**Article**: Wright, S. (1920). The Relative Importance of Heredity and Environment in Determining the Piebald Pattern of Guinea-Pigs. *Proceedings of the National Academy of Sciences*, *6*(6), 320–332. <https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.6.6.320>
